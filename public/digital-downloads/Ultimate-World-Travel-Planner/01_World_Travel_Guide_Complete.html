1. The 7 Golden Rules of World Travel

  • Pack 30% lighter than you think: If it doesn't fit in a 40L carry-on, you don't need it.
  • Always carry two bank cards: Keep one in your daypack and one locked in your luggage.
  • Download offline Google Maps: Never rely exclusively on international roaming in rural zones.
  • Book internal trains & flights mid-week: Tuesdays and Wednesdays are routinely 25% cheaper.
