=============================================================
THE ESSENTIAL TRAVEL PACKING CHECKLIST
=============================================================

[ ] DOCUMENTS & ESSENTIALS
  • Passport / ID (valid 6+ months)
  • Physical copies of passport & visa
  • Travel insurance policy card
  • International credit cards (no foreign transaction fee)
  • Universal power adapter with USB-C ports

[ ] TECH & GADGETS
  • Power bank (10,000 - 20,000 mAh, airline approved)
  • Noise-canceling earbuds / headphones
  • E-reader or loaded tablet
  • Offline downloaded maps & offline translation packs

[ ] CLOTHING (Capsule Wardrobe)
  • 1 lightweight rain/windproof jacket
  • 1 warm fleece / packable down layer
  • 4 breathable quick-dry t-shirts
  • 2 pairs versatile travel pants/chinos
  • 1 pair broken-in walking shoes / trail runners
  • Quick-dry underwear & merino wool socks

[ ] HEALTH & WELLNESS
  • Personal first-aid kit & blister plasters
  • Rehydration salts / electrolyte packets
  • Motion sickness & pain relief meds
  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen & insect repellent
